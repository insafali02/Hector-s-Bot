01. Acceptance of terms

By installing, accessing or using the Network Intelligence Discord bot or this website (together, the “Service”), you agree to these Terms. If you are installing the bot in a Discord server, you must have authority to approve the installation and related permissions.

These Terms apply in addition to the Discord Terms of Service and any applicable rules or laws.

02. What the Service provides

Network Intelligence offers Minecraft Java status checks, public DNS and SRV lookups, IP/ASN and RDAP information, subdomain observations, routing diagnostics, limited TCP connectivity checks, and owner-managed controls such as blocking certain lookups.

The Service is an information and diagnostic tool. Availability, report fields and command access may change or be limited by public data sources, Discord permissions, hosting constraints or operational needs.

03. Authorized use only

You may use the Service for legitimate administration, troubleshooting, learning and research. For active diagnostics such as ping, traceroute and TCP port checks, you must have permission from the owner of the target system or another valid legal basis.

Do not use the bot to harass others, disrupt services, evade network protections, collect information for unauthorized access, or violate Discord/server rules or applicable law. The Service does not provide or authorize DDoS attack launching.

04. Installation and permissions

Server administrators determine whether to install the bot and which Discord permissions or roles it receives. Some commands are restricted to bot owners or Discord server administrators. Global owner-defined blocks and rate limits may apply across servers.

Do not attempt to bypass block rules, command restrictions, access controls or rate limits. We may limit, refuse, suspend or discontinue access to protect users and infrastructure.

05. Data accuracy and limitations

Results can be delayed, incomplete, approximate or outdated. IP geolocation, ISP attribution, BGP data, public certificate records and Minecraft responses depend on external providers and target configurations.

Important: a public SRV or DNS IP may represent a proxy, CDN or public-facing edge rather than a hidden server origin. A server status response does not by itself confirm a private backend IP. Verify critical findings with the system owner and trusted infrastructure records.

06. Third-party platforms and services

The bot relies on Discord and may query public DNS services and third-party data providers to answer commands. Those providers have their own policies, availability and restrictions. The Service does not control third-party content, response accuracy or outages.
